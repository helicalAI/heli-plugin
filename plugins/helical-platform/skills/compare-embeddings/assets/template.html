UMAP explorer

Foundation-model UMAP explorer

Pick a model to see its embeddings, coloured by datasource. Click a legend entry to hide a datasource, double-click it to show only that one. Drag to zoom, scroll to zoom at the cursor, double-click the plot to reset.

Do the datasources mix?

Share of each cell's nearest neighbours that come from its own datasource, computed on the embeddings, not on the plot. Close to chance means the model mixes the datasources; close to 100% means it keeps them apart.
